Built for Banks and Microfinance Institutions

Our team includes an engineer with data engineering experience at microfinance banks. We understand loan files, repayment schedules and month-end reconciliation, and we build tools that support your staff without replacing your core banking system.

Loan operations & monitoring

Applications, documents, approval stages, repayment schedules and overdue alerts in one place.

Collections management

Follow-up assignments, automated repayment reminders and recovery tracking for loan officers.

Automated reconciliation

Transactions matched across statements, payment channels and internal records, with exceptions flagged.

Management dashboards

PAR, delinquency, branch and officer performance, updated continuously instead of monthly.

Document processing

Data extracted and organised from customer documents, application forms and financial records.

Workflow & field apps

Internal approvals and escalations, plus mobile tools for field officers and customer visits.

Financial data, handled with care

Every engagement is scoped around your security, regulatory and procurement requirements.

  • Access to production systems only with your authorisation and agreed controls
  • BVN, NIN and transaction data handled with data minimisation and strict confidentiality
  • People review and approve AI output in sensitive workflows
  • Hosting, backup and recovery arrangements agreed during scoping
