Jollyfy

An event planning marketplace for Nigeria. People find venues, caterers, photographers, decorators and other vendors in Lagos, Ibadan and Abuja, and can plan the whole event by chatting on WhatsApp or Telegram.

Engineered for Financial Institutions, Fintechs & Operations-Heavy Businesses

With deep background in microfinance and banking data engineering, we build secure, compliant systems for banks and MFIs—while extending that same institutional rigor to fintechs, credit providers, and high-volume operations. We streamline reconciliation, credit workflows, and reporting without disrupting your core systems.

Loan & Credit Operations

Applications, documents, approval stages, repayment schedules and overdue alerts in one place.

Collections & Recovery Management

Follow-up assignments, automated repayment reminders and recovery tracking for operations and field officers.

Automated Multi-Channel Reconciliation

Transactions matched across statements, payment gateways, mobile channels and internal ledgers, with exceptions flagged.

Real-Time Executive Dashboards

PAR, delinquency, branch and portfolio performance, updated continuously instead of monthly.

Intelligent Document & KYC Processing

Data extracted, verified and organized from customer documents, application forms and financial records.

Custom Workflows & Field Apps

Internal approvals, escalations, and mobile tools for field officers, customer visits, and operations.

Financial & sensitive data, handled with care

Every engagement is scoped around your security, regulatory compliance and operational requirements.

  • Access to production systems only with your authorisation, agreed controls and audit trails
  • BVN, NIN, customer PII and transaction data handled with data minimisation and strict confidentiality
  • People review and approve AI output in all sensitive or financial workflows
  • Hosting, encryption, backup and recovery arrangements agreed during scoping
